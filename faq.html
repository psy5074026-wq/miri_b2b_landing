FAQ

Good to know

Quick answers about MIRI plant-based Korean vegetable dumplings.

Q.Is it quick and easy to make?

A.Yes — MIRI dumplings go from freezer to table in minutes. Pan-fry, steam, or air fry — no thawing, no prep, no mess.

Q.Is it healthy? What's actually inside?

A.Yes — MIRI is 100% plant-based vegetable dumplings made with tofu, no cholesterol, no meat. Just real vegetables and clean ingredients you can pronounce.

Q.Is it good for game day or a party?

A.Yes — MIRI dumplings are a crowd-pleasing appetizer straight from the freezer. Perfect for game day snacks, parties, or any easy get-together.

Q.Are they actually crispy, or just another mushy veggie dumpling?

A.Yes — MIRI dumplings are genuinely crispy on the outside, savory on the inside. No more mushy, bland veggie dumplings.

Q.What makes it authentically Korean?

A.Yes — MIRI dumplings are yachae mandu, Korea's traditional vegetable-filled dumpling, made the way Korean families have made it for generations.

Authentic Korean dumplings, crafted by Sol Food Co., Ltd. since 1987 — now for the U.S. market.
