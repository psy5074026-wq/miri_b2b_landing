FAQ

Good to know

Quick answers about MIRI plant-based Korean vegetable dumplings.

Q.Is it quick and easy to make?

A.Yes — MIRI dumplings go from freezer to table in minutes. Pan-fry, steam, or air fry — no thawing, no prep, no mess.

Q.Is it healthy? What's actually inside?

A.Yes — MIRI is 100% plant-based vegetable dumplings made with tofu, no cholesterol, no meat. Just real vegetables and clean ingredients you can pronounce.

Q.Is it good for game day or a party?

A.Yes — MIRI dumplings are a crowd-pleasing appetizer straight from the freezer. Perfect for game day snacks, parties, or any easy get-together.

Q.Are they actually crispy, or just another mushy veggie dumpling?

A.Yes — MIRI dumplings are genuinely crispy on the outside, savory on the inside. No more mushy, bland veggie dumplings.

Q.How do I steam dumplings?

A.Boil water in a steamer or pot with a bamboo steamer basket, line it with parchment or cabbage leaves, arrange dumplings in a single layer, and steam 7–9 minutes — straight from frozen.

Q.Wonton vs dumpling — what's the difference?

A.Wontons use a thin, square wrapper and are usually served in soup; dumplings (including Korean mandu and potstickers) use a thicker round wrapper and are steamed, pan-fried, or air fried. MIRI makes Korean mandu — built to crisp up, not go soggy.

Q.What's the nutrition information?

A.MIRI Fresh Vegetable Dumplings are 120 calories per 3-piece serving (80g), with 4g protein and 2g fiber. Full nutrition facts are on each product page.

Q.What's actually inside fried dumplings?

A.miri fried dumplings ingredients are simple: cabbage, onion, tofu, glass noodle, dried radish, soy protein, green onion, garlic chives, sesame oil and garlic, in a wheat wrapper. That is the whole list — so what’s inside fried dumplings here is vegetables and tofu, not filler.

Q.How many calories are in dumplings?

A.A 3-piece serving (80g) of miri Fresh Vegetable Dumpling is 120 calories with 4g protein and 2g fiber. For comparison, steamed pork dumplings calories typically run higher because of the fat in the filling — pan-frying adds roughly 20–30 calories per serving in oil. Fried dumplings nutrition varies mostly by how much oil the pan holds, not by the dumpling itself.

Q.What makes it authentically Korean?

A.They are yachae mandu — Korea's traditional vegetable-filled dumpling, made the way Korean families have made it for generations.

Authentic Korean dumplings, crafted by Sol Food Co., Ltd. since 1987 — now for the U.S. market.
