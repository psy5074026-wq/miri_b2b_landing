Easy Peasy Air Fry

Hands-off, crispy, and mess-free — perfect for a quick snack or appetizer.

  1. Preheat the air fryer to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Lightly brush or spray frozen dumplings with oil.
  3. Arrange in a single layer, not touching. Air fry 12–15 minutes.
  4. Flip halfway through for even browning. Serve hot.
Chef's tip: Leave space between dumplings so hot air circulates — crowding makes them soft instead of crispy.
