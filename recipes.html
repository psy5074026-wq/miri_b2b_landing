Guide

How to use a bamboo steamer

A bamboo steamer is the traditional way to steam dumplings — it keeps the wrapper delicate instead of soggy, since the wood absorbs excess condensation.

  1. Fill a wok or wide pot with 1–2 inches of water — enough to simmer without touching the steamer basket.
  2. Line the basket with parchment paper, napa cabbage leaves, or lettuce so dumplings don't stick to the bamboo.
  3. Arrange dumplings in a single layer with space between each — steam needs to circulate.
  4. Stack & steam — bamboo steamers stack, so you can cook multiple layers at once. Cover and steam frozen dumplings 7–9 minutes.
  5. No steamer? A pot with a metal colander or a heatproof plate set on crumpled foil works the same way.

Quick Answers

Cooking FAQ

Q.How do I steam dumplings?

A.Boil water in a steamer or pot with a bamboo basket, line it to prevent sticking, arrange dumplings in a single layer, and steam 7–9 minutes from frozen.

Q.How do I cook dumplings without a steamer?

A.Pan-fry them — golden on the bottom, then steam-finish with a splash of water under a lid. Or air fry at 375°F for 12–15 minutes.

Q.Can I cook them straight from frozen?

A.Yes — no thawing needed. Steam, pan-fry, or air fry directly from frozen for the crispiest result.

Q.What's a quick dumpling dipping sauce?

A.Soy sauce, rice vinegar, sesame oil, a pinch of sugar, and scallion — ready in under a minute while your dumplings cook.
