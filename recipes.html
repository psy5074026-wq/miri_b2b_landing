How to cook
frozen dumplings.

No thawing, no prep — miri dumplings go straight from freezer to table in minutes. Steam, pan-fry, or air fry, then finish with a quick dipping sauce.

Signature Dishes

Three ways to serve them

Beyond the basics — a party platter, a warming soup, and a fresh salad bowl, all starting from frozen.

20 min · Serves 6–8

Game Day Dumpling Board

The play that never loses on game day: a wooden board piled with golden mandu, three game day appetizer dips, and cold beer. Feeds a room, disappears by halftime.

  1. Pan-fry 30–40 frozen mandu in batches, 8–9 minutes each — golden side down first, then a splash of water under a lid to finish.
  2. Soy-scallion dip: 3 tbsp soy sauce · 1 tbsp rice vinegar · 1 tsp sesame oil · sliced scallion · pinch of chili flakes.
  3. Creamy gochujang mayo: 3 tbsp mayo · 1 tbsp gochujang · 1 tsp honey · squeeze of lime.
  4. Chili crisp oil: 3 tbsp chili crisp · 1 tsp soy sauce · 1 tsp toasted sesame seeds.
  5. Pile the dumplings on a board, tuck the dip bowls alongside, and finish with scallion and sesame. Serve hot.
Make-ahead tip: Mix all three dips up to 3 days early — then it's just 20 minutes of frying before kickoff.
15 min · Serves 2

Mandu-Guk — Korean Dumpling Soup

Korea's everyday comfort bowl, and the answer to how to make dumpling soup with frozen dumplings — no stock to build, no thawing, on the table in fifteen minutes.

  1. Bring 4 cups anchovy or vegetable broth to a boil with 1 tsp soy sauce and a crushed garlic clove.
  2. Add 8–10 frozen soup dumplings straight from the freezer. Simmer 6–7 minutes until they float and the wrappers turn glossy.
  3. Meanwhile, make egg garnish (jidan): beat 1 egg, cook it thin in an oiled pan, then slice into fine ribbons.
  4. Season the broth with salt and a few drops of sesame oil. Top with the egg ribbons, sliced scallion, toasted sesame, and black pepper.
  5. Serve with kimchi and danmuji (pickled radish) on the side.
Plant-based version: Use vegetable broth with miri Vegetable Dumplings and skip the egg — same fifteen minutes.
15 min · Serves 2

Crispy Dumpling Salad Bowl

Proof that crispy dumplings belong on a weeknight dinner plate, not just a snack tray. A lighter, vegetable-forward bowl for anyone eating less meat without giving up texture.

  1. Air fry 4–6 frozen mandu at 375°F for 12–15 minutes until deeply crisp, flipping halfway.
  2. Build the base: baby spinach, shredded red cabbage, julienned carrot, sliced cucumber, and edamame in a wide bowl.
  3. Add sliced avocado along one side for richness.
  4. Sesame-soy dressing: whisk 2 tbsp soy sauce · 1 tbsp rice vinegar · 1 tbsp sesame oil · 1 tsp honey · 1 tsp toasted sesame seeds.
  5. Lay the hot dumplings across the greens, spoon over the dressing, and finish with sesame and micro greens.
Why it works: Dress the greens first and add the dumplings last — the wrapper stays crisp instead of steaming into the salad.

Quick Answers

Cooking FAQ

Q.How do I steam dumplings?

A.Boil water in a steamer or pot with a bamboo basket, line it to prevent sticking, arrange dumplings in a single layer, and steam 7–9 minutes from frozen.

Q.How do I cook dumplings without a steamer?

A.Pan-fry them — golden on the bottom, then steam-finish with a splash of water under a lid. Or air fry at 375°F for 12–15 minutes.

Q.Can I cook them straight from frozen?

A.Yes — no thawing needed. Steam, pan-fry, or air fry directly from frozen for the crispiest result.

Q.What's a quick dumpling dipping sauce?

A.Soy sauce, rice vinegar, sesame oil, a pinch of sugar, and scallion — ready in under a minute while your dumplings cook.
