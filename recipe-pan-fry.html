Crispy Golden Pan Fry

Golden, crackling edges with a hot, juicy center — the classic way to enjoy miri dumplings.

  1. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a non-stick pan over medium heat.
  2. Place frozen dumplings flat-side down. Fry 2–3 minutes until the bottoms turn golden.
  3. Add 3 tbsp water and cover immediately. Steam 4–5 minutes until the water evaporates.
  4. Uncover, drizzle a little oil, and crisp for 1 more minute. Serve hot.
Chef's tip: Don't move the dumplings while the bottoms brown — that's what creates the signature crispy crust.
